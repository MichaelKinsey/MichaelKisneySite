03 · Workflow

Four steps

From a blank landscape to eroded terrain with painted layers.

Enable

Turn on the Landmass plugin and enable Edit Layers on your landscape actor.

Add

In Landscape mode, open Manage › Blueprint and add MK_ErosionBrush.

Tune

Pick a preset or adjust scale, strength, octaves and gully weight. Save your own presets for later projects.

Paint

Name a landscape layer, choose slope, ridge, deposition or altitude, and pick Add, Replace or Multiply.

06 · Questions

FAQ

Setup, every parameter and known issues live in the docs. Read the docs →

Is this a simulation?

No. Erosion is generated procedurally, so it appears immediately instead of running hundreds of iterations. You can move or remove the brush at any time.

Does it change my landscape permanently?

No. It's a Landmass brush on an edit layer, so the original landscape stays untouched underneath.

Do I need Nanite?

No. It works on regular landscapes.

Which engine versions are supported?

UE 5.8 for v1. Version 1.1 adds UE 5.6.

Can I texture the erosion?

Yes. The splat mask output marks gullies and ridges so your landscape material can layer rock, sediment or grass on them.
