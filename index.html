About

Tools I needed, built properly

I'm Michael Kinsey, a technical artist working across the Unreal Engine 5 rendering stack. I build terrain, water and surface systems that hold up up close and cast real shadows, without relying on Nanite.

I prefer building systems myself to understand them, then turning what works into tools other teams can use. Many of them are free. Away from the engine I shoot wildlife, street and travel photography.
